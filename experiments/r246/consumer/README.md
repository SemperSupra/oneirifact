# Actual aquatic native consumer

This child consumes immutable R246 source binds and authored aquatic intent from [PR 102](https://github.com/SemperSupra/oneirifact/pull/102), commit `2e93628bbda838887bd51133789aca56e31f3a08`, [public run 46153095988](https://github.com/SemperSupra/oneirifact/actions/runs/46153095988), artifact 15266855834, ZIP SHA256 `e694ab4d06bae33bf829e624d03a2bd81bb28d8b8059c42ae83e36819f33dd68`. Source preparation was pinned to PR 99 commit `7fc8141fef8e2133ecceb2474005e82da4da69ad`. The source-body and limb/skin transport helpers adapt the already exercised R245 native consumer; the ground implementation and aquatic core remain unchanged.

Two measured binds, male155flat and female185flat, are explicitly consumed barefoot. Their names retain historical source-case labels; no flats, stilettos or aquatic-footwear qualification transfers. No source geometry/topology, source bone identity, rest binding or full skin weights are changed by this consumer. Original source licenses accompany the products.

The original 163-bone armature receives rigid segment transforms from separate native two-joint controls, preserving the `.02` intermediate bone subdivisions. Aquatic root/head rotations and knee/elbow poles use their explicit world semantics. Already resolved world hands are never moved toward a mouth/head proxy. Both original clips receive every authored 24 fps sample, including fractional terminal times; native actions, saved-scene replay and body-only GLB reimport also receive fractional-seek checks.

The consumer observes original-bone FK, actual skin sole subsets, actual source chest vertex identity/position, actual head/mouth proxies and a rigid wrist-parented sponge mesh center. Foot heel/forefoot/toe values are explicitly rigid original-joint FK landmarks, not a claim that those virtual points coincide with deformed skin contacts. Every source vertex index is checked against original local coordinates before and after reimport. Native and saved-scene observations include evaluated world vertices of the actual floor, seat, rim, water surface and bottom meshes. Their agreement with declared context and observed limb residuals is reported separately from physical contact. The body-only GLB explicitly omits native context. Separate numerical body transport, observation critic, context diagnostics and realism states are retained.

The saved .blend contains actual animated source skin, editable named actions, construction controls, a static water surface/bottom, a tub floor/rim/seat shown with omitted walls for review, and a rigid sponge proxy. These original context meshes are native/render evidence and are excluded from the animated body GLB. Water, sponge and tub targets do not establish fluid forces, buoyancy, balance, friction, grip, seat/skin contact, washing effectiveness, breathing or airway clearance. Internal anatomical structures remain omitted.

Public standard CPU jobs use pinned Blender 5.2.4 LTS, Node24 actions, exact checked-out heads, read-only permissions, maximum parallelism two and a 15-minute cap. Actual stills and short MP4s are rendered from the native geometry. The explicit SHA256-verified imageio-ffmpeg 0.6.0 wheel avoids the earlier public-runner missing-encoder failure. Every MP4 is fully decoded and source-frame count/duration plus first/middle/last lossy image errors are checked. The images are prototype evidence for evaluation, not realistic-motion or biomechanical acceptance.

`native_critic.py` and `native_oracles.py` are independent reviewer contributions. They consume recorded actual observations without importing the motion compiler or Blender solver, preserve explicit incomplete/omitted states, and run controlled corruptions of measured observations. `qualification.py` records product completeness and bounded numerical results after media and critic receipts are written; upload remains unconditional on failure.
