Reproduce or modify the study: parameter profiles · original scene recipe · model exporter. The Blender files retain the render rig; GLB exports omit the evaluation ground, camera and lights. No product engine or third-party assets are included.

Project direction

Explore a design, adjust its parameters, compose reusable components, and keep an editable creation. The intended browser experience is lightweight; richer local applications can use more of a device's resources.

Release notes and instructions will identify what is actually supported as the project develops.
