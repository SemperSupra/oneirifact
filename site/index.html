Native Oneirifact artifact gallery — not yet published

Native entries will provide the editable feature graph, component definitions and assembly scene where applicable, together with native generation receipts and matching rendered and exported geometry. A Blender recipe or mesh alone does not qualify.

Project direction

Explore a design, adjust its parameters, compose reusable components, and keep an editable creation. The intended browser experience is lightweight; richer local applications can use more of a device's resources.

Release notes and instructions will identify what is actually supported as the project develops.
